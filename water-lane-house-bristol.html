Water Lane House, Bristol

A central Bristol iQ property focused heavily on self-contained studio living, with communal facilities including a roof terrace.

Public information checked 13 October 2026. Selected rooms currently show cashback up to £600 and limited availability.

Current studio examples

iQ currently advertises Gold Studio rooms from £358/week and Platinum Studios from £369/week on the 2026/27 room pages we checked.

Useful guarantor detail

iQ says no guarantor is needed if rent is paid in a single instalment. For payment by instalments, a guarantor is required. Its current guidance says guarantors can be UK or internationally based, must be 21 or over, and cannot be a full-time student or current iQ resident.

Facilities

  • Communal social and study spaces
  • Studios with private bathroom and kitchen
