Croft Court, Bristol

A brand-new, modern PBSA on Stokes Croft with premium studios and en-suite rooms, high-quality communal spaces and bills included.

Current offer: up to £1,000 cashback on selected rooms. Public pricing checked 25 October 2026. Fresh currently advertises Croft Court from £240/week to £410/week for 2026/27, depending on room and contract.

Current public pricing

Fresh lists 44- and 51-week options. A Premium Studio is currently advertised at £355/week for 51 weeks or £360/week for 44 weeks.

Why Croft Court stands out

It is one of Bristol’s newest student accommodation schemes, with a more premium, design-led feel than many older PBSA buildings. The combination of brand-new rooms, central Stokes Croft location and current cashback makes it especially worth comparing if you want something modern rather than simply the cheapest option.

What you get

  • Studios and en-suite rooms
  • All bills included
  • Wi-Fi and broadband
  • Gym, cinema room, study room and games area
  • Courtyard and social spaces
  • On-site residents' team and CCTV

Who it may suit

Students who want to live in central Bristol and value being surrounded by Stokes Croft's independent food, nightlife and shops rather than living on a quieter campus-style site.

What to check before booking

Compare the total contract cost, exact room size and floor, current incentives, and your real journey to campus. Studio prices can vary meaningfully by room grade.
