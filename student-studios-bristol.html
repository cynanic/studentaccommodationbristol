Student studios in Bristol

A studio buys privacy, but the price gap versus an ensuite can be significant. Compare what you get for the extra money rather than assuming “studio” automatically means better.

What counts as a student studio?

Usually a private bedroom, bathroom and kitchenette contained within one unit. Facilities and layouts vary substantially between buildings, so floor area, storage, kitchen equipment and natural light are worth checking.

Compare these before you book

  • Total tenancy cost: weekly rates can hide differences in contract length.
  • Floor area: two studios at the same price can feel completely different.
  • Kitchen: check hob, oven or combi microwave, fridge size and prep space.
  • Laundry: check whether it is included or charged separately.
  • Location: calculate the actual route to university.
  • Social space: studios vary hugely in access to communal areas.
