Budget guide

Cheap student accommodation in Bristol

The lowest weekly headline price is not necessarily the cheapest tenancy. The useful number is what you will actually pay from move-in to move-out.

Compare the whole cost

Multiply the weekly rate by the contract length, then account for bills, transport, deposits and any genuine cashback. A slightly higher weekly price with a shorter contract or included utilities can sometimes cost less overall.

Ways to reduce the cost

  • Compare shared bathrooms as well as ensuites.
  • Look beyond the most expensive central locations.
  • Check whether a longer commute creates meaningful transport costs.
  • Compare contract lengths carefully.
  • Treat cashback as a discount only after checking the eligibility terms.
  • Do not pay for facilities you know you will never use.
