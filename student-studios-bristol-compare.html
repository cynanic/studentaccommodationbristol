Before choosing a Bristol student studio

Compare the total rent over your tenancy, not just the weekly headline. A 44-week studio and a 51-week studio can have very different total costs. Cashback and promotional gifts may be limited to particular room grades or booking dates.

Choosing a student studio in Bristol

A studio normally provides a private kitchen and bathroom, unlike an en-suite bedroom with a shared kitchen. Compare studios with other studios, not the cheapest room in each building. Check the contract year and length before relying on a weekly headline.

For a dated 2027/28 example, The Propeller Quarter Deluxe Studio Courtyard View was listed at £231/week for 51 weeks (£11,781 total) or £235/week for 44 weeks (£10,340 total). These are specific grade prices, not a Bristol-wide studio minimum. Compare verified next-year prices and budget alternatives.
