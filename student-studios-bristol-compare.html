Compare student studios in Bristol

Compare properties advertising student studios in Bristol. Where room-grade prices are recorded, the displayed starting rate is for a studio, not the building’s cheaper en-suite rooms. Properties without a verified studio price appear afterwards. Rates shown relate to 2026/27 unless explicitly stated otherwise; check contract length, availability and offers with the operator.

Before choosing a Bristol student studio

Compare the total rent over your tenancy, not just the weekly headline. A 44-week studio and a 51-week studio can have very different total costs. Cashback and promotional gifts may be limited to particular room grades or booking dates.
