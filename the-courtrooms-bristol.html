The Courtrooms, Bristol

A centrally located Yugo-managed residence that is exclusive to University of Bristol students and is booked through the university accommodation office rather than directly with Yugo.

What stands out

  • Exclusive to University of Bristol students
  • Very central location
  • Common room, study rooms and courtyard
  • Water, gas and electricity included
  • High-speed internet and 24-hour security listed by Yugo

Booking route

Yugo currently directs students to the University of Bristol Student Accommodation office to secure a place. This is important because it is not a standard direct-booking PBSA in the same way as many commercial properties.

Guarantors

Yugo says a guarantor is required when accommodation is paid in instalments, with the guarantor liable if the resident cannot make the payments.
