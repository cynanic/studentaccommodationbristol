Methodology

How we compare student accommodation

The aim is simple: make Bristol accommodation easier to compare without pretending every building is identical.

What we look at

  • Advertised weekly price and total contract cost.
  • Room type and published room size where available.
  • Contract length.
  • Included utilities and facilities.
  • Published incentives such as cashback.
  • Location and practical journey to universities.
  • Provider terms that materially affect the booking.

Where the information comes from

Property information is drawn from publicly available operator and property sources. Prices and availability can change quickly, so students should always verify the final offer directly with the provider before booking.

Commercial relationships

If this site later receives referral fees, affiliate commission or payment for sponsored placement, that relationship will be clearly disclosed. Paid placement will not silently become an editorial recommendation.
