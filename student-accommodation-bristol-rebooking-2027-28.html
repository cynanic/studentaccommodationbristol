Returning residents

Bristol student accommodation rebooking 2027/28

Existing residents often get first access to the next academic year before general sale. This page will track who has opened rebooking, what the deadlines are, and whether there are incentives for staying.

What to check before you rebook

  • Whether you can keep the same room
  • The deadline for priority access
  • Whether the new weekly price has changed
  • Any rebooker-only cashback or vouchers
  • Deposit and guarantor requirements
  • Whether your contract rolls into a different length
  • When the room is released to general sale if you do nothing

Why timing matters

Rebooking periods can be short. Popular room types may move quickly once general sale opens, so compare the new price and terms before assuming staying put is automatically the best deal.
